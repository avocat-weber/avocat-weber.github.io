LEGAL + TECHNICAL CONTEXT

Legal workflows are not generic business workflows.

Legal automation works best when the person designing the technology understands documents, evidence, confidentiality, review processes and the consequences of incorrect output.

My work sits between software engineering, artificial intelligence and legal workflow design, with particular attention to explainability, source traceability, permissions and human review.

APPROACH

AI that survives contact with real work.

Useful before impressive

Automation should save measurable time or improve quality—not merely demonstrate that an LLM can produce text.

Sources over hallucinations

Where factual reliability matters, systems should retrieve authoritative material and expose their sources.

Human review by design

High-impact workflows should make review and validation easy rather than pretending human judgement is obsolete.

Privacy matters

Architecture should reflect confidentiality, permissions and the sensitivity of the information being processed.
